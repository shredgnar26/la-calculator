## EXPAREL interpretation gates

The retrieved PI is revised 11/2023; DailyMed's record updated 2025-12-12. Verification date is distinct from revision date.

1. **Ratio:** manufacturer examples allow 150 mg HCl with 266 mg EXPAREL or 75 mg with 133 mg using salt/free-base equivalence. Candidate instead allows 133/66.5 mg, literally applying listed mg 1:2. This is a deliberately stricter **app restriction**, not a claim that every manufacturer example violates FDA labeling. Clinician/pharmacist must resolve the intended interpretation before release.
2. **Dilution:** 266/300=0.886666… rounds to 0.89; manufacturer expansion example uses 300 mL. Candidate exact 0.89 floor allows at most 298.876…mL total for 266 mg. This is stricter than rounded 1:14. No silent rounding or universal mixture approval. [Manufacturer example](https://www.exparel.com/hcp/EXPAREL_Fast_Facts.pdf).
3. Exact perineural/adductor regimens, no extra adductor diluent, no epinephrine adjunct in the adductor regimen, and one site are conservative scope controls; they do not establish that every other clinical practice is contraindicated. Labeled additional analgesics remain a separate clinician decision.
4. Evidence for off-label fascial plane/other blocks is not implemented as FDA dosing. No formula assigns a dose for these uses.

## Numerical policy

1 lb=0.45359237 kg exactly (international avoirdupois pound); percent w/v=10 mg/mL per 1%. Intermediate values are not rounded. UI displays up to 3 decimal places for doses/volumes and fewer for broad fluid ranges; raw inputs govern checks. Small 1e-9 floating comparison tolerance only. Missing and malformed values fail closed. Software boundaries do not validate plausible adult physiology. Same-drug entries are summed against the strictest entered limit regardless of status; separate procedures/repeat doses need external review.
